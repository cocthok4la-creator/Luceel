About Luceel

Small, plain tools that do one job well.

Luceel makes simple web tools that respect the people using them. Each one does a single job, runs in your browser and asks for nothing you do not need to give.

Luceel Resume exists because resume builders have become paywalls with templates attached. The goal is the opposite: tools that are free to use, honest about what they can and cannot do, and do not keep your personal details.

Luceel Mark is the same idea for QR codes: free, clean and made in your browser, with no account and no watermark. Luceel Size does it for file sizes: it makes photos and PDFs fit the limit a form asks for, without uploading them.

If something does not work, or you want a feature that is missing, write to us from the help center below. Real messages shape what gets built next.

    No watermarks, trial timers, upsell screens or tracking scripts on these pages.

  • Honest tools

    Checks say what they measure. A pass is not a promise, and the page says so.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about building, checking and saving your resume.

Is it really free?
Yes. Building and downloading do not require an account, and there is no watermark or download limit.
Where is my resume stored?
In your browser, on this device only. Clearing your browser data removes it, and other browsers or devices will not see it. Use “Export JSON” in the builder as a backup, and import it anywhere.
Does a good ATS check guarantee I will pass?
No. Every employer’s software is different. The check shows the text a parser reads and flags common problems such as missing contact details, missing dates or unusual characters. Treat it as a guide.
Does it use AI?
No. Job match, the writing coach and the ATS view are simple rules that run on your device. They point out issues and ask questions, but they never write or invent content for you.
How do I save a PDF?
“Download PDF” opens your browser’s print window. Choose “Save as PDF” as the destination and turn off “Headers and footers” for a clean page. The text stays selectable, so parsers can read it.
What does the QR code on my resume do?
You choose: open your portfolio or LinkedIn page, save your contact card to a phone, or open a shareable copy of the resume. A density meter tells you whether the code will scan at the printed size.
How does the share link work?
The resume is packed into the link after the “#” sign. Browsers do not send that part to a server, so nothing is uploaded. Anyone with the link can read the resume, and very long links may not work in some chat apps. Use the PDF when in doubt.
Can I use it on my phone?
Yes. Editing works on small screens. Saving a PDF depends on your phone’s print options, so a computer is easier for the final download.
Why are there different page sizes?
The US and Canada use Letter. Most other countries use A4. Pick the one your employer expects, or let the regional preset choose.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about making, testing and printing your code.

What can I put in a QR code?
Here you can encode a web address, plain text, email, phone number, Wi-Fi details, WhatsApp chat, UPI payment, contact card, social profile, map location or calendar event.
Are these QR codes free to download?
Yes. Downloading PNG, SVG and PDF files here doesn’t require an account.
Can a QR code stop working?
The code itself doesn’t expire, because it contains your content directly. It can stop being useful if the website it points to goes away, or if the printed code gets damaged or smudged.
Why should I test the code before printing it?
Devices and scanner apps differ. Test at the size you intend to print, on the real background, with more than one phone if you can.
What file format should I use for print?
SVG is the safest choice because it stays sharp at any size. If you use PNG, keep it at its original size or smaller rather than enlarging it.
Does changing the colors affect scanning?
It can. Keep a dark code on a light background with strong contrast. Light codes on dark backgrounds and low-contrast pairs fail on some scanners, and the page warns you when contrast is low.

    Questions

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Your photos and PDFs are processed inside your browser and are never sent to a server. They are kept in memory only while this page is open. To make PDFs smaller, the page loads a PDF reader library from a public code host the first time you use that tool.

    Why is my result a little under the size I asked for?

    The tool looks for the best picture quality that stays at or under your limit, and file sizes do not move in perfectly even steps. If a form also needs a minimum size, fill in the minimum field for JPEG photos.

    Is 1 KB 1,000 or 1,024 bytes?

    Websites differ. The maximum size here uses 1,000 bytes per KB and the minimum uses 1,024, so a file that fits your range also fits sites that count the other way. Your computer may show a slightly different number for the same file.

    How does the minimum size work?

    For JPEG photos, the tool can add invisible padding so the file reaches your minimum. The picture itself does not change. This is not available for WebP or PNG.

    Why can I not select text in a PDF after making it smaller?

    To reach a small size, each page is saved as a picture. That keeps the page looking the same but removes the text layer. Keep your original file if you need the text.

    Which photo types can I use?

    JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP work in all modern browsers. Some browsers can not open iPhone HEIC photos. If yours can not, share or export the photo as JPG first.

    Can I get an exact size like 200 × 230 pixels?

    Yes. Choose “Set an exact width and height”, then pick whether to crop the photo to fill that size or fit it inside while keeping its shape.
