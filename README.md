# Luceel

Free, simple web tools that run in your browser: no sign-up, no installs.

**Live site:** [luceel.in](https://luceel.in)

## Tools

- **Resume builder:** build, tailor and export resumes (PDF, Word, text) in many languages, with an ATS check, job match and a built-in job tracker.
- **Luceel Mark:** make clean, scannable QR codes for links, Wi-Fi, WhatsApp, UPI payments and more, then download as PNG, SVG or PDF.
- **Luceel Size:** resize photos, turn images into a PDF, or shrink a PDF to the size you need.

## Privacy

Your files and resume are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to a server.
